74 `<item>` entries served `<enclosure url="…" type="…">` with **no `length`
attribute**. RSS 2.0 requires an enclosure byte length, so feed validators flag
the feed and some readers skip the artwork. The channel also had no
`<atom:link rel="self">`, which leaves aggregators without a feed discovery hint.

### Change (commit `dd7cebd`, pushed 08:16Z)

- `src/pages/rss.xml.js`: `enclosureXml()` now stats the local asset and emits
  its real byte size (falls back to the spec-valid `0` when the asset is not a
  local file). Added `xmlns:atom` and an `atom:link rel="self"` to the channel.
- `scripts/check-seo.mjs`: fails when any `<enclosure>` lacks a numeric
  `length`, and warns when the atom self link is missing or duplicated.
