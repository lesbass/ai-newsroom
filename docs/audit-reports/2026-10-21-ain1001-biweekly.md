## Executive summary

The site is in good shape: 13/13 automated checks pass against the live host, the 158-URL sitemap crawls clean, metadata and structured data are complete, and there is no horizontal overflow or console error at either breakpoint.

Two small mobile type issues were visible in real Chromium screenshots and were fixed in this run:

1. The 113-character MultiLangBench headline rendered at 35.2 px across **8 lines** on a 375 px phone. Long article headlines now scale down on mobile (28.1 px, **6 lines**) without dropping below the h2 hierarchy.

A third density fix, already landed on this issue as `7d863ca`, keeps card tag chips to two labels plus `+N` on mobile so every listing entry uses one chip row instead of spilling `+N` onto a mostly empty second row. It is verified live here.

No new implementation issue is needed. The remaining observations are editorial/performance notes, not site defects.

## Technical SEO

- **Canonical/base URL:** every checked page, sitemap entry, RSS link/guid, OG URL, and JSON-LD URL uses `https://news.lesbass.com/`. No `ai-newsroom.pages.dev` or `ai-newsroom.workers.dev` links.
- **Titles:** rendered title lengths are within the 70-character budget. The newest article's `<title>` is exactly 70 characters and truncates on a word boundary; its full 113-character headline remains the `h1` and `og:title`.
- **Descriptions:** the newest article carries a 149-character description; all listing pages carry unique descriptions.
- **Open Graph/Twitter:** `og:title`, `og:description`, absolute `og:image`, Twitter card, and image alt are present on all checked page types.
- **JSON-LD:** `WebSite` on home/listing pages; `NewsArticle` + `BreadcrumbList` on the article page. All blocks parse as JSON.
- **Images:** the article hero has descriptive alt text, width/height, source URL, capture date, credit, and license note; no image is missing alt text.
- **Indexing:** no robots blocks, no noindex, no canonical conflicts, no redirect chains on sitemap URLs.
- **Tooling correction:** `scripts/check-seo.mjs` counted HTML entities (`&#39;` = 5 characters) as title/description length. It now decodes entities first, so the warning is based on rendered characters. This removed the only recurring false warning.
