# AIN-1030 — Related articles on every article page

**Date:** 2026-10-22
**Scope:** Internal linking only. No canonical, sitemap, RSS, JSON-LD, or date changes.

## Change

- Extended the existing `src/lib/topicTags.ts` (which already owns
  `TOPIC_MIN_ARTICLES` and `getTopicTagSlugs()`) with `getRelatedArticles()`.
  The selector receives the `getTopicTagSlugs()` set, so related rows use
  exactly the same indexability rule as the topics index, the tag route's
  `noindex` check, the topic pills, and `qualifyingTagSlugs` in
  `sitemapLastmod.js`. Tags carried by only one article are excluded before
  ranking.
- `getRelatedArticles()` picks up to three other articles that share at least
  one indexable tag, ranked by shared-tag count and then `pubDate`.
- `/articles/[id]/` now renders a compact `Related articles` list after the
  article body and Sources block. Rows show the article title, date, and the
  shared topic labels as plain text (no tag links). The section is omitted when
  there is no qualifying candidate.

## Result

- 74 article pages built; 74 render a related section.
- 73 pages carry 3 related links; `x86-ace-specification-ai-compute-extensions`
  carries 1 because `amd` is its only tag shared with another indexable tag set.
- 0 self-links, 0 related links outside `/articles/`, 0 tag links inside the
  related section.
