## Fix — mobile article header density (`8c02fac`)

Two defects were visible on every article page at 375 px:

1. The metadata row stranded a separator at the end of a line
   (`… 4 MIN READ ·` / `10 SOURCES`).
2. The breadcrumb's current-page crumb repeated the full H1 as two lines of muted text
   immediately above the headline.

Change:

- Each metadata separator is now grouped with the value it introduces, so a wrapped line
  cannot leave the dot behind.
- The redundant `AI Newsroom` author item is hidden below 600 px (the masthead already names
  the publication); non-brand authors stay visible.
- The current-page breadcrumb truncates with an ellipsis instead of reflowing the full title.

Verified in Chromium at 375×812:

- metadata is one line: `OCTOBER 13, 2026 · 4 MIN READ · 10 SOURCES`
- breadcrumb is one truncated line
- `scrollWidth == clientWidth == 375` (no horizontal overflow)
- desktop 1280×800 layout unchanged
