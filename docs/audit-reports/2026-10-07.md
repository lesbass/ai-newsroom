## 3. Visual judgment (desktop + mobile screenshots)

Screenshots committed under `docs/audit-reports/2026-10-07/` — `home/`, `articles/`, and
`articles-alberta-claude-466m-lines-government-code-2026/`, each with
`screenshot-desktop.png` (1280×800) and `screenshot-mobile.png` (375×812), captured from
`https://news.lesbass.com/` on 2026-10-07 with `scripts/screenshot.mjs`.

**Desktop**

- Masthead, nav and hero read as one system; the tick rail under the logo aligns with the logo's left
  edge. Nav active-state underline is clear.
- Featured card and the `/articles/` list are typographically strong: serif headline, mono metadata
  line, tag pills, two-line dek. Listing density on `/articles/` is good — 3 cards per screen, each
  card's dek clamped by `truncateCardDesc()` at 220 characters.
- Article page: breadcrumb → headline → meta → tags → lead image → prose, all on one 760px column.
  No overlap, no clipping, no horizontal scroll (`documentElement.scrollWidth === clientWidth` at
  1280 and 390).

**Mobile (375×812 / 390×844)**

- Nav wraps onto its own row instead of overflowing; nothing overlaps or is cut off.
- Tag pills collapse to 4 + `+N`, so no pill row wall (regression check for AIN-847).
- Article cards are scannable; `/articles/` is bounded at 20 cards per page (AIN-868) — 4 pages for
  66 articles, each in the sitemap.

**Observation recorded, not changed this heartbeat**

1. **Two left edges on the homepage at desktop widths.** The hero's headline starts at ~132px while
   the content column starts at 280px (1280px viewport): header/footer/hero use
   `width: min(1120px, 100% - 2rem)` while `.container` uses `max-width: var(--max-width)` = 760px.
   Both are centred, so nothing is broken, but the hero hangs left of everything below it. A fix would
   need a matching change across `.home-hero` padding, `.container` and the mobile padding floor —
   worth its own focused issue rather than a drive-by edit. Filed as **AIN-881**.
   **Follow-up (2026-10-07 00:54 UTC):** fixed and deployed in `7b98005` — headline, `.section-label`
   and cards now share one left edge at 1280/1024/768, mobile unchanged, live audit 10/10. Full
   numbers and screenshots in `docs/audit-reports/2026-10-07-ain881-hero-align.md`.

**Fixed this heartbeat**

2. **`GET /favicon.ico` returned 404.** The site declares `<link rel="icon" href="/favicon.svg">`, so
   browsers were fine, but clients that hardcode `/favicon.ico` (feed readers, crawlers, older Safari)
   got a 404. Added a rewrite to `public/_redirects`:

   ```
   /favicon.ico /favicon.svg 200
   ```

   Commit `b14e6b4`, pushed to `main`. Verified on production at **2026-10-07 00:31 UTC**:
   `GET /favicon.ico` → **200 `image/svg+xml`** (was 404). Re-verified after the deploy:
   `test:seo` ✅ and `test:links` ✅ against `https://news.lesbass.com/`, plus 200s on `/`,
   `/sitemap.xml`, `/rss.xml`, `/articles/`, `/articles/page/2/`, `/tags/`, `/corrections/`, a 404 on
   a bogus path, and the `http://` → `https://` **301** still intact.

Neither observation was a publication blocker.
