## Findings

### F1 — `dario-amodei-pace-the-frontier` ships with no image (editorial, follow-up opened)

Frontmatter has `image: ""`, so the article page renders no `<img>` and `test:images` warns:
"editorial policy requires at least one image per article". Needs a real image, or the documented
`image: exception` sentinel if it is intentionally image-free.

Related: `scripts/check-images.mjs` does not special-case `image: exception`, so the two articles
that legitimately use the sentinel (`mozilla-thunderbolt-privacy-first-ai-client`,
`openai-astra-pacing-cyber-critical-monitoring`) also warn. The warning list therefore cannot
distinguish a genuine gap from an intentional exception.

### F3 — long article headlines push the hero below the fold (design note)

`clamp(2.2rem, 6vw, 4.45rem)` at 1366px produces a five-line `h1` for long titles, so the
breadcrumb + headline alone fill the first screen and the hero image/dek fall below the fold.
Suggested mitigation: cap the display size by headline length (e.g. step down above ~45 chars).
